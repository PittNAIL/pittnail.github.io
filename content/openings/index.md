<h3 class="service-role">PhD Training Philosophy</h3>
<span class="service-org">From close mentorship to research independence</span>
</div>
<div class="service-body">
<p>My philosophy for PhD training is to guide students progressively from close mentorship to research independence through three phases, as shown in the figure below.</p>
<h4>Three Phases</h4>
<p><strong>Phase 1: Fully Supervised.</strong> I work closely with students throughout the research lifecycle, including identifying important questions, reviewing the literature, developing solutions, designing experiments, and writing their first papers.</p>
<p><strong>Phase 2: Semi-Independent.</strong> Students take increasing responsibility for literature review, solution development, experimental design, and manuscript preparation while continuing to receive regular feedback.</p>
<p><strong>Phase 3: Fully Independent.</strong> Students are expected to identify significant and feasible research questions, conduct rigorous studies, and write, submit, and revise papers with minimal supervision.</p>
<img src="/pic/phd_pathway.png" alt="PhD training pathway: from fully supervised to fully independent" class="phd-pathway-img" />
<p>The goal is to prepare each student to become a confident and independent researcher and scientist who can make meaningful contributions to the field.</p>
<p class="service-note">If this training philosophy aligns with your goals and you are motivated to grow through these phases, I encourage you to apply to one of the programs and consider joining our lab.</p>
</div>
